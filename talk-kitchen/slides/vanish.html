Rule 4 · Nobody gets lost

If a cook vanishes, the ticket goes back on the rail

1

Cook 2 takes the risotto, and calls out “still on it” every few minutes.

2

Cook 2 goes quiet: crashed, or ran out of time.

3

The ticket returns. The next cook finds the half-made risotto and carries on.

Rule four. Cooks drop out. An AI assistant can crash, lose its connection or simply run out of its time budget. In a normal kitchen, that ticket would hang there forever with a name on it. In ours, every cook calls out “still on it” every few minutes. If a cook goes silent for too long, their ticket automatically goes back on the rail. And here’s the important part: the half-finished work isn’t thrown away. It stays on the counter, labeled, so the next cook picks up exactly where the last one stopped. Nobody has to notice, nobody has to clean up.
