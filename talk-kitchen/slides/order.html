Cooks only take tickets that are ready, and the one that unblocks the most dishes comes first.

Rule two. You can’t plate the pasta before the sauce exists. So each ticket says what it waits for. When a cook is free, they don’t ask anyone what to do. They look at the rail, see which tickets are ready right now, and take the one that holds up the most other dishes. Here that’s the sauce: two other tickets are waiting for it. Nobody has to hand out work. The cooks pull it themselves, which is also how many factories have worked since the 1950s.
