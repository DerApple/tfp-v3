The problem · 1

One AI assistant is like one very fast cook

Fast

A dish in minutes that would take a person a day.

Tireless

Works through the night, never gets bored.

Forgetful

Starts every shift with no memory of yesterday.

You’ve probably heard of ChatGPT or similar tools. The newer ones don’t just chat: they can do work on their own. We call them AI agents, but let’s just call them cooks. One cook on its own is impressive: very fast, never tired. But notice the third card. Every time a cook starts, it remembers nothing from before. Everything it needs to know has to be written down somewhere it can read. Keep that in mind, it matters later. And with one cook and one dish, that’s all easy. The trouble starts when you want a whole dinner.
