Where people fit

People run the kitchen. The AI does the cooking.

Write the menu

Decide what gets made, and what “good” means.

Read the notes

Confirm or overrule the choices cooks made alone.

Answer the call

Step in when a cook is truly stuck.

Shout “Stop!”

Pause new tickets while the menu changes.

So where do people fit in? Everywhere that matters. People write the menu: what should be built and what counts as good. People read the notes and keep or overrule decisions. People answer when a cook is genuinely stuck. And at any moment a person can shout “Stop!”: no cook takes a new ticket while we rethink the plan, and anything no longer wanted can be crossed off. My own job has changed: I spend much less time cooking and much more time writing good tickets and checking results. That’s the real shift: from doing the work to directing it and judging it.
