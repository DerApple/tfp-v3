Everyone looks at the same rail. If it isn’t on a ticket, it doesn’t exist.

Rule one. Every piece of work is written on its own ticket, and all tickets hang on one rail that every cook can see. Remember that our cooks are forgetful: the rail is their memory. Each ticket says exactly what “done” means, like a recipe. For the curious: in real life the rail is a shared folder of small text files, one per job. It keeps a complete logbook of every change, who made it and when, so nothing is ever lost. Programmers call that tool Git, but you don’t need to remember the name.
