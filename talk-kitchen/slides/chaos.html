The problem · 2

Ten cooks and no system: chaos

Two cooks make the same soup

Double the work, one pot wasted.

Nobody makes dessert

Everyone thought someone else had it.

A half-raw dish reaches a guest

“Done” was said, not checked.

Nobody knows what’s ready

And the chef is asleep.

Now hire ten of these cooks and tell them: make a five-course dinner. Without a system you get exactly what you’d get with ten people: two make the same soup, nobody makes dessert, someone serves something half-raw because they said “done” and nobody checked, and nobody can tell you what’s actually ready. Ask the audience: who has worked in a team where this happened? Hands usually go up. That’s the point: this isn’t an AI problem, it’s a teamwork problem. And with AI it’s worse, because the cooks are so fast that the chaos happens in minutes.
