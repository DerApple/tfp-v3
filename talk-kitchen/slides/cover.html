Order #1

Explain what I do to people who don’t cook code

A talk about working with AI

The kitchen that cooks while I sleep

How a team of AI assistants works together, and where people fit in

Peter Max Gürtler

Welcome. I’m going to tell you about my work, and I promise: no code, no jargon. Instead, we’re going to spend the next half hour in a restaurant kitchen. By the end you’ll understand how I get a team of AI assistants to build things together, overnight, without me standing next to them, and why the most important part of it has nothing to do with technology.
