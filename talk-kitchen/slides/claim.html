Rule three. A cook who takes a ticket writes their name on it, and only one name fits. That alone prevents the “two cooks, same soup” problem. What if two cooks grab the same ticket at the exact same moment? The rail decides: exactly one of them wins, and the other gets a clear “sorry, taken” and simply goes back to the rail for the next one. There’s no boss in the middle handing out work and no meeting. The rail itself is the referee.
