A vague menu, vague food

Writing good tickets is now the hard part, and it’s human work.

I want to be honest about the limits, because that’s where trust comes from. First: the cooks make mistakes, sometimes confident ones. The whole system is built around that, with taste tests and notes, but it doesn’t make mistakes disappear. Second: the automatic test proves a dish is safe to serve, not that it’s what the guest wanted. That judgment stays with people. Third, and the most surprising lesson for me: the result is only as good as the tickets. A vague menu gives vague food. Writing clear tickets has become the most important part of my job.
