Here’s the good news: busy restaurant kitchens solved this problem more than a hundred years ago. A good kitchen doesn’t depend on brilliant cooks; it depends on a few simple rules everyone follows. Every order is a ticket. All tickets hang on one rail everyone can see. And a head chef tastes every plate before it goes out. What I built is exactly that, for AI cooks. It’s called TFP, the Task Flow Protocol. “Protocol” just means: rules everyone agreed on. In the next few minutes I’ll show you the six rules. Each one is something you already know from a kitchen.
