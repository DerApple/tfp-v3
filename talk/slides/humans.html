Agents decide and record

Pick the most reasonable option, then log it:

tasks.py assume <id> "Used bcrypt; spec names no hash"

Humans steer, not assign

assumptionsReview decisions later
unholdAnswer a parked question
freezeNo new claims while replanning
dropThis is no longer wanted
This line is straight from the agent rules. Agents should never block on a human. When something is unclear, they choose the most reasonable option and record it as an assumption, one line per decision a human might want to revisit. Humans then review assumptions in batch, answer held questions, freeze the board while replanning, or drop work that is no longer wanted. The human role shifts from assigning work to shaping the graph and auditing decisions. I haven't seen this treated as a first-class concept elsewhere.
