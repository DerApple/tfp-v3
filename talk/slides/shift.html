The shift

Doing the work is getting cheap.
Coordinating it is not.

Ten agents can write code in parallel today. The hard part is making sure they work on the right things, don't collide, and leave something the next one can use.

The cost of executing a well-defined task is collapsing. What is not collapsing is the cost of deciding what to do, splitting it, keeping parallel workers from stepping on each other, and recovering when one of them dies halfway. That is the problem this talk is about: coordination, not execution.
