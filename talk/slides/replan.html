When workers are LLMs · 2

Agents may reshape the plan

split

Too big? Write 2 to 6 subtasks. The parent waits and later only integrates.

new

Discovered a missing task? Add it with its deps.

hold

Needs a human decision? Park it with the question.

extend

Nearly done? One more session, once.

Guardrails: subtasks can't be split again; two sessions per task, then a review.

The task graph is not fixed. Agents are often the first to notice that a task is too big, or that something is missing. So they may split a task into a handful of subtasks, add new ones, or put one on hold with a precise question for a human. To keep this from running away, there are hard limits: subtasks can't be split again, and after two sessions on one task the runner asks for a review. In tuple-space terms, workers also produce tuples; the interesting part is governing that.
